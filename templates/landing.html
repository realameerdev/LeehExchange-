Support & reliability

Clear when it matters.

Every order follows a visible path, with live rates, admin verification and Telegram updates along the way.

How do I start a trade?+
Open the LeehExchange Telegram bot, choose a service, and follow the guided steps. No separate exchange app is required.
How long does a transaction take?+
Orders are reviewed and processed as quickly as possible during operating hours. You receive a Telegram notification and email receipt when your order is confirmed.
What assets and gift cards are supported?+
LeehExchange supports a growing range of crypto assets plus popular US and UK gift cards. Choose a service in the bot to see the available options.
Can I get help while trading?+
Yes. Use Venora Flash on this page for quick answers, or reach the LeehExchange team through the Telegram experience.
